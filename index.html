          Banana Peanut Butter

          Our classic creamy, nutty everyday blend.

          Smoothie5 minBeginner

          Ingredients & amounts

          • 1 medium ripe banana
          • 1 cup (240 mL) milk of your choice
          • 1 tablespoon (16 g) peanut butter
          • ½ cup (120 g) plain or vanilla yogurt (optional)
          • Protein powder, optional — use the serving size on its label
          • 4–6 ice cubes, if you like

          How to make it

          1. Add the milk and banana to the blender.
          2. Add peanut butter, yogurt, and optional protein powder.
          3. Blend until smooth. Add ice or a splash more milk to adjust.
